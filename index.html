About Us

Over 10 Years of Excellence in Dental Restorations

Sharaf Dent Lab has been at the forefront of dental prosthetics for over a decade, delivering exceptional quality to dental clinics across the region. Our state-of-the-art facility is equipped with the latest CAD/CAM milling technology, 3D printing systems, and precision tools.

Our team of master ceramists and dental technicians brings years of specialized experience in crafting zirconia crowns, E.max veneers, implant-supported prosthetics, and complex full-arch rehabilitations.

We are committed to building trusted partnerships with dentists, providing direct clinical support, custom shade matching, and rapid turnaround times.
